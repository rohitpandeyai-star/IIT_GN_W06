# How are the 40 marks given?

The mini-project carries 40 of the build week's 100 marks: 24 for the team and 16 for each member. Every line below is scored by counting what is present, so two scorers reach the same mark. The panel and the TAs score on the same sheet.

## Which team marks are there, out of 24?

| Code | Criterion | Marks | One mark for each of these |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Frame and metrics | 5 | (1) The unit is the night, with the reason. (2) The walk ceiling sits inside the money rule. (3) The wrong number in the brief is caught, with its checked value in the charter. (4) The north star and the guardrail each have a numerator, a denominator, a window and exclusions. (5) The euro figures carry the full-night caveat, with the share of nights near full at each hotel. |
| T2 | Honest evidence | 5 | (1) A time split, with the proof period run once. (2) Every column binned in `docs/COLUMNS.md`. (3) The client's flat rule, a lookup table and at least two learned models, all scored by `src/contract.py` on the same nights. (4) Every number on the slides reproduced by one command. (5) No late column and no random split anywhere in the chosen rule. |
| T3 | The client's three observations | 4 | (1, 2, 3) One mark per observation answered with its witness: the number that explains it. (4) All three answers changed something in the build, and the slides name what. |
| T4 | The MVP | 4 | (1) The MVP command runs live on Saturday. (2) It writes the nightly table for the fourteen nights. (3) It reads only first-bin columns, signed by the challenger. (4) The architecture diagram matches the code. |
| T5 | Evidence discipline and the AI storyline | 6 | (1) No slide restates the brief. (2) Every number on every slide has its screenshot. (3) The grilling slide shows three rows that changed a document. (4) The comparison names up to three gaps and up to three AI mistakes, each with a file reference, or says "none found" and names the file that shows it. (5) The roadmap has three weeks, each with an owner and hours. (6) `ai_logs/INDEX.md` lists every log, including the four required rows. |

## Which individual marks are there, out of 16?

| Code | Criterion | Marks | How it is counted |
|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | The recording | 6 | One mark for each of the five parts present and specific, and one mark when the cited commit hash and chat log verify. Missing, under ten minutes, or narrated without the screen scores 0. |
| I2 | Defence in questions | 6 | Two questions: one on your own number, one on a teammate's. Each scores 3 for a right answer with the mechanism, unaided; 2 after one prompt; 1 for the right number without the mechanism, or with a teammate's help; 0 for no answer. |
| I3 | The trail | 4 | (1) Commits under your name on your artifacts. (2) Your chat logs indexed. (3) Every AI-written line the panel points to, explained. (4) Your challenger signature given on your partner's artifact. |

## What is never scored?

Which method wins. Two teams with opposite winners can both take full marks for evidence, because the marks go to the discipline of the comparison. Clues taken from a TA cost nothing.
