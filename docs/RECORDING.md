# What goes in my recording?

Every member records one screen-share of 15 to 20 minutes about their own work. Camera is optional and no editing is needed. It is due on Friday, before the end of the build day.

## Which five parts, in which order?

| Part | Minutes | What you show |
|---|---|---|
| 1. My artifacts and the question I owned | 2 | The files you own, opened, and your role's question in your own words |
| 2. The number I own | 5 | The line of code that produces it, a run that prints it, and what it means for the client |
| 3. Where I was stuck | 4 | What you tried, what you expected, what you saw, and how you got out, with the commit or log that shows it |
| 4. My research, with proof | 3 | The source or chat you used, its file in `ai_logs/` or its link, and the commit hash where it changed the work |
| 5. What I would do differently | 2 | One decision you would remake, and why |

## Where does the link go?

Upload the recording to the LMS and paste the link next to your name in the README.

## What will the TA check?

The TA opens your recording at three points, confirms that you are the one speaking, and checks that the commit hash and the chat-log file you cite exist in the repository under your name. Under ten minutes, or narrated without the screen, scores zero.
